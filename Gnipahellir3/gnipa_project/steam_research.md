# Steam Early Access research, 2026-11-02

For card 85. Read-only web research by an agent, mostly Valve's own pages, nearly all undated.
Check again before acting on any date. P = https://partner.steamgames.com.

## Confirmed or corrected

- Fee: $100 per app, non-refundable, recouped after $1,000 Adjusted Gross Revenue. P/doc/gettingstarted/appfee
- Fee to release: Valve disagrees with itself: 30 days (P/steamdirect), 21 days (onboarding page). Plan on 30.
- Coming Soon: at least two weeks, public. P/doc/store/releasing
- Refund: within 14 days AND under 2 hours played. Early Access play counts. store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds
- Trap: launching in Early Access counts as released, Next Fest takes only unreleased games, and each game gets one Next Fest. So Next Fest must come before Early Access.

## Lead times

- Partner paperwork: 15-30 days. Tax check is "up to 30 days" or "2-7 business days" by page; plan 30. Store page work waits for verified bank, tax and company information.
- Store page review: 3-5 business days, submit at least 7 business days ahead. P/doc/store/review_process
- Build review: same. Allow one failure cycle. Store page goes in first. You press Release yourself.

## Onboarding, Norway

- Individual: "Sole Proprietorship" with your legal name as Company Name; bank holder name must match.
- W-8BEN. No treaty: 30% withheld on the US-sourced share. A treaty rate needs a W-8BEN with a TIN. Withholding already taken is not refunded, so get the TIN right the first time.
- Norway treaty rate of 0% is UNCONFIRMED (low-quality secondary source only). Ask an accountant or Skatteetaten. Norwegian income tax and MVA were not researched.
- Inference: enkeltpersonforetak onboards as Sole Proprietorship with the national ID as foreign TIN; an AS would likely need W-8BEN-E.
- VAT: Valve collects and remits; Norway listed at 25% inclusive.
- Paid in USD by SWIFT, monthly, about 30 days after the sales month, $100 minimum.

## Store page

- Capsules: Header 920x430, Small 462x174, Main 1232x706, Vertical 748x896. Older half-size figures are out of date.
- Library: 600x900, hero 3840x1240, logo. Whether required is unconfirmed; prepare them.
- Capsules carry only art, name and official subtitle. Screenshots: at least 5, 1920x1080, gameplay only.
- Trailer required, showing gameplay. mp4 H.264/AAC, up to 1920x1080.

## Early Access rules (P/doc/store/earlyaccess)

- Must be playable, not a tech demo, not already finished, not crowdfunding.
- "Do not make specific promises about future events."
- Disclose known breaking changes such as broken saves.
- Price no higher than any other store. No discount within 30 days of a price increase.
- You cannot return to Early Access or Coming Soon. Full launch visibility comes only at 1.0.
- Dropping the game: Valve offers keeping it with Early Access status removed, or removal. Notice after 12 months without a build.
- Demo alongside Early Access: unconfirmed; no page addresses it.

## AI disclosure (P/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey)

"Efficiency gains through the use of these tools is not the focus of this section. Instead, it is concerned with the use of AI in creating content that ships with your game, and is consumed by players. This includes content such as artwork, sound, narrative, localization, etc."

- Pre-generated: content shipped and consumed by players, made with AI tools. Disclose.
- Live-generated: made while running; also needs guardrails described.
- Code is never mentioned. Inference, medium confidence: AI-assisted code is outside it. Any AI-made sprites, audio, lore, in-game text or translations must be disclosed, so audit those.
- Open question: does "Let an agent play" (card 84) count as anything here? The player's own agent generates nothing that ships. Not researched.

## Money

- Revenue share: 30% standard (secondary source); 25% past $10M, 20% past $50M, per application.
- Launch discount at most 40%, 7-14 days. No other discount for 30 days after release. Seasonal sales need release at least 30 days before.

## Platforms

- No page read requires a non-Windows build; no page states Windows is mandatory. Windows plus Proton looks like the minimum.
- Native Linux depot optional. Deck: smallest font at least 9 px at 1280x800, a real check for a pixel-art UI.
