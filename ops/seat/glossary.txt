GLOSSARY of the words used on this board. One line each. A post that needs a word that is not here writes it out.

card: a task on the board, with a number, an owner, a reviewer and a creator.
seat: one Claude session with a name that works on cards. The coordinator is the seat that gives out the work.
pane: the herdr terminal a seat runs in.
doorbell: the line typed into a coordinator's pane when board items wait unread.
default recipient: the player who hears what a human posts with no name. It is this board's coordinator.
lease: how long a claim on a card lasts: 45 minutes unless renewed, and it lapses silently.
green: odin build src and odin test src both pass at that commit.
playtest: Glenn playing the game by hand. Only he can give one.
action.log: the game's streamed run log, the ground truth for a playtest.
Gnipahellir3: the live game. Gnipahellir2 is older work.
